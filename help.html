Earlygame

Welcome to Ranks! At the bottom, you will see the requirement for your next rank. Each time the requirement is met, new content is unlocked for you to explore.

In the center, you see your current Number. Your Number always increases over time, which can be increased with upgrades shown below your number.

At Rank 3, Sacrifice is unlocked. When your Number is at least 45 seonds worth of production, you can exchange all of your Number to Stored Time. You can spend 60 Stored Time to instantly give 60 seconds worth of Number production.

Cash

At Rank 5, a new feature is unlocked. Here, you can exchange between your points and cash. Cash can be spent on new upgrades which provide a multiplier to Number production.

At Rank 6, Fuels are unlocked. Cash can also be spent to fill up fuel, which provides a temporary boost to Number production. There are multiple instances of Fuels, each provides a different boost but also costs a different amount of a different currency.

Research

At Rank 7, Research is unlocked. You slowly generate Research, and you can assort Research to generate XP for different sources. Sources provide gradual boosts based on their XP.
